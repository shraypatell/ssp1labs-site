How we work

Every application we publish is built and maintained in-house. We do not sell personal information and we do not carry advertising SDKs. The third-party services our apps rely on — for authentication, purchase handling, and crash diagnostics — are named individually in each app’s privacy policy.

Questions, bug reports, and refund requests all reach a person. Write to support@rallystudy.app and you will get a reply.

Support

For help with Rally Study, email support@rallystudy.app, or read the support and FAQ page. Include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem — it makes the fix considerably faster.

We answer within one to two business days.
